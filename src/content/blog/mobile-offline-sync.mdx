## Constraints

Field teams cannot depend on stable connectivity.

## Sync strategy

- Local-first writes with durable queue.
- Retry with exponential backoff.
- Conflict merge based on last-write plus domain-specific rules.

## Monitoring

Track queue depth and failed replay rate as first-class reliability metrics.
