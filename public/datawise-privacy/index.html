DataWise Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026
Summary (in plain English): DataWise reads how much internet data each app on your phone uses, so it can display it to you, forecast your monthly usage, and notify you about limits you set. All of that data stays entirely on your phone. We never see it, and it is never uploaded to any server. The free version displays ads from Google AdMob, which may collect standard device identifiers to serve and measure ads. You can permanently remove all ads with the one-time Premium purchase.

What the app reads, and why

With your explicit permission (Usage access, which you enable in Android Settings), DataWise reads from Android:

  • How much mobile data and Wi-Fi each app and the device as a whole have used (via Android’s NetworkStatsManager).
  • How often each app was opened (via Android’s UsageStatsManager), to spot "data hogs" that consume data in the background but are rarely opened.
  • The display names and icons of your installed apps, so you can easily identify them.

This information is processed strictly on your device. It is never transmitted to us or any third party.

Data stored on your device

DataWise persists the following locally on your phone:

  • A daily snapshot of data consumed per app, enabling accurate forecasts and month-by-month comparisons.
  • Data bundles you record (size, duration, and optional price) along with balance corrections.
  • Data limits you set and notification states already triggered.
  • Apps you selected to block on mobile data (Premium).
  • Your Premium purchase status.

All of this data is private to the application sandbox and is completely deleted when you uninstall the app.

Notifications

DataWise runs a lightweight background check approximately every 15 minutes to evaluate your limits (notifying you at 80%, 100%, and when exceeded), alert you when your bundle is running out, and deliver a monthly summary on the 1st. All evaluation takes place locally on your phone.

Blocking apps on mobile data (Premium Firewall)

If you choose to block an app on mobile data, DataWise creates a local loopback VPN using Android’s standard VpnService API. Only the specific apps you choose to block are routed into this tunnel, where traffic is intentionally dropped. DataWise never inspects, records, modifies, or transmits your network packets, and unblocked apps bypass the tunnel entirely. You can unblock apps at any time, and Android displays a persistent key icon in the status bar while the firewall is active.

Checking your balance via USSD (Premium)

When you tap "Check with this code" in the balance dialog, DataWise executes the balance code you entered (e.g., *124#) through Android’s telephony layer. This only occurs when you explicitly initiate it in the foreground. The carrier’s response is parsed locally to update your bundle count and is never transmitted off your phone.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob (banner ads and occasional interstitials when saving settings). Notifications never contain ads.

To serve, personalize, and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • Your device’s advertising ID
  • IP address and approximate coarse location
  • Diagnostic device information (device model, operating system version, app version)
  • Ad interaction metrics

We do not receive any identifiable information from these queries. Learn how Google processes advertising data here: Google Partner Policies.

Your choices:

  • EEA / UK / Switzerland: Consent is requested via Google’s User Messaging Platform before personalized ads are served.
  • All users: You can reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time in Android system settings.
  • All advertisements can be permanently disabled via the one-time Premium purchase.

In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

The optional Premium upgrade is processed directly by Google Play Billing. We never collect or store your payment cards or financial credentials. Google Play only confirms purchase entitlement to unlock features. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Permissions used

  1. Usage access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS): To read data consumption per app and launch frequencies. Granted manually by you in system settings.
  2. Query all packages (QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES): To resolve system UIDs to human-readable app names and launcher icons.
  3. Post notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): To alert you before or when you exceed your set data limits.
  4. Make phone calls (CALL_PHONE): Only required if you choose to use the USSD carrier balance checker.
  5. VPN (BIND_VPN_SERVICE): Used only if you block apps on mobile data (Premium).
  6. Internet: Used exclusively for Google AdMob and verifying Google Play billing status. Your personal usage data never touches the internet.

DataWise does not access your contacts, SMS messages, precise location, camera, microphone, photos, or files.

Children's privacy

DataWise is a general-purpose utility designed for general audiences (13+) and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your control and rights

You retain complete control over your data. You can revoke Usage access in Settings at any time, delete custom data limits, reset your Advertising ID, or uninstall the app to erase all stored metrics. Under GDPR, CCPA, or applicable data laws, you have the right to request deletion; because DataWise never transfers your personal data to any external server, no records exist outside your physical device.
